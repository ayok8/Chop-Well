OUR STORY

Chopwell Kitchen started with a simple belief: good food should feel like home. We're here to bring authentic Nigerian flavours to your table — one delicious meal at a time.

From a home kitchen to your table

What began as a small family kitchen in Lagos is now Chopwell Kitchen — a place where every pot tells a story. We grew up surrounded by the aroma of simmering stews, the sizzle of peppered chicken, and the joy of sharing a meal with people you love.

That's the feeling we want to pass on to you. Every dish we make is prepared the way our mothers and grandmothers taught us — with patience, with care, and with a whole lot of flavour. Nothing is rushed, nothing is fake. Just real Nigerian food, made the right way.

And because a great meal deserves a great drink, we squeeze our fruit juices fresh daily. Watermelon, orange, pineapple, beetroot — whatever you're craving, we've got it. All fruits available, always.
